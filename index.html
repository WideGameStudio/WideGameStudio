About us

Discover
Wide Game Studio

We are Wide Game Studio, a passionate team dedicated to pushing the boundaries of virtual reality. Our mission is to deliver immersive VR gaming experiences that captivate people and spark inspiration.

We believe in the power of imagination and technology to create immersive worlds that transport users to magical places. Our vision is to be at the forefront of the VR industry and setting the trends by crafting innovative projects that redefine the future of virtual reality.
