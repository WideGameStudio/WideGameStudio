1. What Types of Personal Information Do We Collect?

"Personal Information" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked to you or your household. Depending on the Game or Service you use, we may collect the following categories of Personal Information:

a. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account and Profile Information: When you create or link a Platform account (for example a Meta account) to play a Game, we may receive your username, display name, profile picture, email address (if provided to the Platform), age range and other account details shared by the Platform.
  • Contact Information: If you contact us for support, feedback or inquiries (e.g., via email, Discord or in-game tickets), we collect your name, email address and any other details you provide.
  • Payment Information: For optional in-app purchases (e.g., cosmetics, bundles, virtual currency), payments are processed through the Platform's payment system or third-party processors. We do not store your full payment card information; instead, we receive transaction confirmations and identifiers.
  • User-Generated Content: Information from in-game chats, emotes, reports, suggestions or shared content (e.g., player names in reports, bug descriptions with screenshots or videos).
  • Surveys and Feedback: Responses to optional surveys, playtests, beta testing feedback or community events.

b. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and Usage Data: Device type, operating system, IP address, browser type, unique device identifiers, language preferences and crash reports. For VR Games, this may include headset model, controller inputs and session duration to optimize performance and detect issues.
  • Gameplay Data: Progress metrics (e.g., levels, items, upgrades and achievements), in-game actions (e.g., sessions completed, interactions with other players) and performance statistics, used to improve our Games and provide features like saving progress, leaderboards or matchmaking.
  • Analytics Data: How you interact with the Services, such as pages visited on our website, time spent in a Game, features used and referral sources.
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address (not precise geolocation) for compliance, fraud prevention and region-specific content. We do not collect precise location data unless you opt in for a specific feature.
  • VR-Specific Data: Aggregated motion and interaction data (e.g., movement patterns, hand and controller motion) to enable and enhance gameplay. We do not collect biometric data such as eye tracking unless it is explicitly enabled and consented to.

c. Information from Third Parties

  • Platform Integration: Account details, friends lists and social interactions shared via a Platform's APIs (e.g., Meta), subject to your privacy settings on that Platform.
  • Partners and Service Providers: Data from analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Unity Analytics, GameAnalytics), advertising partners or anti-cheat services.
  • Public Sources: If you link social media accounts (e.g., X, Instagram), we may collect public profile information.

We do not collect sensitive Personal Information such as racial or ethnic origin, health data or biometric identifiers without your explicit consent, and only if necessary for a Game feature.

2. How Do We Collect Your Personal Information?

  • Directly from You: Through account creation, in-game inputs, support tickets or voluntary submissions.
  • Automatically: Via cookies, pixels, logs, software development kits (SDKs) and similar technologies on our website and in our Games. For VR Games, this includes headset sensors for basic functionality.
  • From Third Parties: As described above, primarily from Platforms for account linking and payments.
  • Inferred Data: We may derive insights from collected data, such as preferences based on gameplay patterns.

You can control some collection through device settings, Platform account preferences, or by opting out of certain features.

3. How Do We Use Your Personal Information?

We use Personal Information for the following purposes:

  • Providing and Improving the Services: To operate our Games (e.g., matchmaking, saving progress), process transactions, customize experiences and fix bugs.
  • Communication: To send updates, patch notes, event notifications or respond to inquiries. With your consent, we may send marketing emails.
  • Analytics and Research: To understand usage trends, optimize performance and develop new features and Games.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: To detect cheating, enforce rules (e.g., bans), protect against unauthorized access and comply with legal obligations.
  • Personalization: To recommend content, match players or display in-game offers based on your activity.
  • Legal Compliance: To respond to legal requests, enforce our terms or protect rights.
  • Advertising: Where a Game contains ads, to show non-personalized ads or, with your consent, personalized ones based on interests.

We do not sell your Personal Information. Any processing is based on legitimate interests, consent, contract performance or legal requirements.

4. How Do We Share Your Personal Information?

We share Personal Information with:

  • Service Providers: Vendors for hosting, analytics, payment processing, advertising and support tools, bound by confidentiality.
  • Platforms: For account integration, payments and compliance with Platform policies (e.g., Meta's Developer Data Use Policy).
  • Partners: For collaborations (e.g., influencer or community events), with your consent.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law, subpoena, or to protect safety.

We do not share Personal Information with third parties for their own marketing without your consent.

Where a Platform allows younger users (for example, Meta's parent-managed accounts for users aged 10–12), those accounts must be created and managed by a parent or guardian under that Platform's requirements.

  • COPPA Compliance: We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we discover such collection, we will delete it.
  • Parental Controls: Parents can manage child accounts via the Platform's tools (e.g., Meta's parental supervision tools), including data access and deletion requests.
  • Data Practices for Children: We limit collection to what is necessary for gameplay.
  • No Targeted Advertising: We do not show targeted advertising to children under 13.

For Meta Platforms, see Meta's Parent Privacy Disclosure: www.meta.com/legal/quest/parent-privacy-disclosure. If you believe we have collected a child's data without consent, contact us immediately.
