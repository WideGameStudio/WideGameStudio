Next to tier up

Creators that will tier up

Bait creators earn Catch Points: 1 for your own idea, 2 for an Idea Board idea. Anglers need videos and views to become Captain. Reach the goal and a Reviewer moves you up on the next tier-up day: every Wednesday.

New rule

Angler → Captain has changed

We changed this rule to avoid spam content and give you a fresh challenge. To go from Angler to Captain you now need:

  1. At least 10 videos posted as an Angler, and
  2. one of these view goals (easiest first):
    • 6 videos with 5,000+ views each
    • 2 videos with 15,000+ views each
    • 1 video with 30,000+ views

Catch Points don't count for Angler → Captain anymore. Bait → Angler stays the same.
